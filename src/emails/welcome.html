Welcome to Irregular Pearl. A classical music knowledge platform for classical musicians — performers, teachers, and students preparing real repertoire, piece by piece.
Every piece gets a living page: structural landmarks grouped by movement, editions compared at the measure level, and performer’s notes from people who have played the work. Paired with a personal library that tracks what you are preparing, what you have performed, and the reflections you jot between takes.
It is non-profit, community-driven, and free forever. Glad you are here.
