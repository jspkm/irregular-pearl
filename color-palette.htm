DZ1Danger zone

DZ2Delete account

DZ3Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Everything you have published under your byline is erased — performer's notes, interpretive schools, signed piece descriptions, structural landmarks, pending drafts, contribution requests, library data, votes, profile, and account record.

Wiki-edited reference data and your past edits to other contributors' content stay in place — but your name is replaced with former contributor on every byline and audit trail.
